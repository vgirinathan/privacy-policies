Monthly Budget Planner • Privacy Policy

What happens to your financial data, in plain terms

Monthly Budget Planner is a 100% offline, local personal budget manager and expense tracker. This page explains exactly what the app does — and does not — do with your data.

The short version

  • Monthly Budget Planner has no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud servers. It runs 100% locally on your device.
  • The monthly income, budgets, categories, and expenses you enter never leave your phone — there is nowhere for the app to send them.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or tracking SDKs are present in the app.
  • The app is completely ad-free and requires zero network permissions.

1 Your financial data

When you enter your salary income, choose a budget template, set spending limits, or log expense transactions, Monthly Budget Planner saves everything directly into an isolated, high-performance local database on your phone (Isar DB). Monthly Budget Planner never scans your phone's external storage, photos, contacts, or location.

All calculations — including budget score analysis, savings goal progress, category totals, and month-over-month trends — happen entirely on your device's processor in real-time.

2 App preferences & storage

Monthly Budget Planner remembers a few things locally in your device's private app storage — nothing here is transmitted anywhere:

  • Budget Settings — salary pay date, monthly income, and selected template distribution.
  • Categories & Budgets — custom category names, icons, colors, and assigned limits.
  • Expense History — expense title, amount, date, category tag, and notes.
  • App Settings — theme preference (Light/Dark/System) and chosen currency symbol.

This data lives only in Monthly Budget Planner's private application sandbox (/data/user/0/com.gibriapps.monthlybudgetplanner). It is permanently deleted if you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

3 Permissions

PermissionStatusWhat it's for
INTERNET Not requested Monthly Budget Planner does not declare network permissions. The app cannot make any internet or network requests.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Not requested The app does not access your photos, files, or external storage.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Not requested The app does not request or track your device location. Currency is auto-configured without location permissions.

4 Advertising & analytics

Monthly Budget Planner is 100% ad-free and telemetry-free. There are no advertising SDKs (such as AdMob), no analytics tracking tools (such as Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics), and no third-party crash report SDKs embedded in the app.

5 Children's privacy

Monthly Budget Planner is a general-audience personal finance tool, not directed at children under 13, and does not collect personal information from anyone — children included, since it does not collect personal information from any user in the first place.

6 Retention & deletion

Since Monthly Budget Planner does not collect or store your data anywhere but your own device, you maintain total control over your data. Everything described in Section 2 stays in the app's local storage until you clear it yourself — via Android Settings > Apps > Monthly Budget Planner > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.

7 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Effective" date at the top of this page will be updated. For any update that affects data handling, we will also note it clearly in the Play Store release notes.
