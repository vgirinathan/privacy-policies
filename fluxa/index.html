The short version

  • Fluxa has no accounts, no sign-in, and no external servers. Everything runs entirely on your device.
  • Your app list, favorites, Spaces, search queries, and launch activity never leave your phone — there is nowhere for Fluxa to send them.
  • Zero sensitive permissions: Fluxa does not request Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS), Accessibility Service, notification access, contacts, SMS, or location.
  • Zero analytics, zero tracking SDKs, zero cloud AI APIs, and zero advertising.

1. Installed applications

As your Android home launcher, Fluxa queries the operating system's standard LauncherApps service to discover launchable applications installed on your device, display their icons and labels in the app drawer, and launch them when selected.

This discovery happens purely locally in real time. Fluxa never uploads, synchronizes, or transmits your list of installed applications to any external server.

2. Preferences & Spaces

Fluxa stores your personal launcher setup locally using Android's on-device DataStore and Room database:

  • Spaces & Space Apps — custom spaces you create (e.g. Home, Work) and apps you explicitly assign to them.
  • Favorites — apps you pin for quick access.
  • Category Overrides — manual category adjustments you make for specific apps.
  • Launcher Settings — theme mode (light/dark/system), accent color, icon size, grid density, gestures, and UI visibility options.

This configuration data stays in Fluxa's private on-device storage sandbox.

3. Personal Intelligence & launch history

Fluxa includes a local-first, deterministic ranking engine that adapts search and space ordering based on your direct interactions inside Fluxa. This operates under strict privacy and data minimization rules:

  • What is recorded: Only direct launches performed within Fluxa (via Search, Drawer, Favorites, Spaces, or Categories) with a local timestamp.
  • What is NOT recorded: Fluxa does not monitor background usage, other apps, notifications, screen content, or system activity.
  • Bounded retention: Launch history is capped at a maximum of 100 entries, and entries older than 30 days are automatically pruned.
  • User control: You can toggle "Use local launch history" off at any time in Settings > Personalization, or instantly wipe all recorded activity via "Clear launch history."

4. Permissions

Permission / RoleWhat it's for
HOME / LAUNCHER Allows Fluxa to act as your default home screen, respond to the Home button/gesture, and launch applications.
<queries> (Launcher) Standard Android package visibility query that lets Fluxa discover launchable activities. Fluxa does NOT use broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.

Fluxa requires no network permissions (android.permission.INTERNET is not declared), no location permissions, no contact permissions, and no device monitoring services.

5. Advertising & analytics

Fluxa contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking telemetry. We do not profile users, track usage, or monetize your personal data.

6. Children's privacy

Fluxa is a general-audience Android launcher. It does not knowingly collect, transmit, or request personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Backup & cloud transfer

If you use Android's built-in system backup (Google Backup / Device Transfer), your layout preferences (Spaces, Favorites, theme, and grid settings) can be preserved when you switch phones.

Behavioral launch history is explicitly excluded from backup and device transfer rules to ensure your daily activity signals remain strictly private on the original device.

8. Retention & deletion

Because Fluxa does not collect or transmit data to any external server, there is no data stored on our end to delete. You have complete control over all data on your device:

  • Clear launch history directly in Fluxa Settings > Personalization > Clear launch history.
  • Wipe all app data via Android's Settings > Apps > Fluxa > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling Fluxa immediately deletes all locally stored preferences, spaces, and history.

9. Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the "Effective" date at the top of the page will be updated accordingly. Any significant change will be detailed in the app's Google Play Store release notes.
